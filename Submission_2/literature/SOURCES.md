## Bibliographic cautions

MCUNet is cited with its first three author names and “et al.” rather than treating the abbreviated repository citation as a complete author list. TinyTL has an earlier title in its own README (“Reduce Memory, Not Parameters”); the later title used here appears in the same authors’ tiny-training README under Related Work. DORY is cited by its author-record 2021 publication year and DOI without guessing final issue, volume, or page numbers. TinyHAR author initials are retained as supplied in the laboratory record.

## Historical verification procedure

For each DOI/title, use the institution’s Scopus subscription to locate the indexed record, confirm the title and authors, record its EID, and verify the source’s coverage for the publication year. A familiar publisher, a DOI, a preprint, an author repository, or a journal’s general indexing claim is not enough to establish that a particular paper is covered. Replace or supplement any unconfirmed entry before claiming compliance.
